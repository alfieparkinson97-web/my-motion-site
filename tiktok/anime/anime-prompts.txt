kits4u · "THE DIVE": anime-style TikTok · prompt pack
=====================================================

WHAT IT IS
A 25-30 second, 9:16 hand-drawn anime short. A teenager kicking a ball about in a park at sunset
imagines a World Cup night, scores a flying diving header, and comes back to the park.
The ending says the new kits drop 27 Oct.

WHICH TOOL
Use any AI video tool that does image-to-video: Kling, Hailuo (MiniMax), Runway, Pika, Sora or Veo.
It's best to work in 2 steps, so the character looks the same in every shot:
  1) Make a STILL image of each shot with an image tool (ChatGPT/DALL·E, Midjourney, Ideogram, Leonardo).
     Paste the STYLE + CHARACTER blocks into every prompt.
  2) Turn each still into a 5-second clip with the tool's image-to-video. Use the MOTION line for that shot.
Settings: 9:16 portrait, 5 s per clip, highest quality, camera motion "auto" unless the shot says otherwise.

TIPS
- Don't name real players, brands or studios ("in the style of Studio Ghibli" etc.). Many tools block it,
  and you don't want a real player's face or a brand logo in an ad. The STYLE block below describes the look instead.
- AI tools are bad at text. Leave all text out of the prompts and add it in CapCut or TikTok,
  or send me the clips and I'll add it.
- If a clip comes out wrong, re-run it with the same image. Small changes to the MOTION line help more than rewriting everything.

═════════════════════════ EDITING IT TOGETHER ═════════════════════════
- Put the clips in order: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7. Trim each to the times above (around 4 seconds).
- Cut 2→3 on a beat drop. Slow down shot 4 even more (to 50%) for the dive.
- Add a hook caption over shot 1 for the first 2 seconds: "wait for the dive 😳"
- Sound: a cinematic or emotional build that explodes on the goal works best. In TikTok search
  "anime", "cinematic" or "epic" (Business account: only Commercial sounds).
- Or send me the 7 clips and I'll cut them on the beat, add the text and the ending,
  and make an original soundtrack with crowd and goal sounds.
